Name: Avocado Toast
Cuisine: American
Time: 5 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: vegan, dairy-free, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 2 slices sourdough bread
- 1 ripe avocado
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1/4 teaspoon chilli flakes
- Sea salt and black pepper
- 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
- Optional toppings: cherry tomatoes, microgreens, sesame seeds, everything bagel seasoning
Instructions:
1. Toast the bread slices until golden and crisp.
2. Halve the avocado, remove the stone, and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
3. Add lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Mash with a fork to desired consistency — chunky or smooth.
4. Spread the mashed avocado generously over the toast.
5. Drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle chilli flakes, and add any optional toppings.
6. Serve immediately.
Notes: Choose an avocado that yields to gentle pressure for perfect ripeness. Lemon juice prevents browning and brightens the flavour. This recipe is ready in under 5 minutes — the fastest in the collection.
