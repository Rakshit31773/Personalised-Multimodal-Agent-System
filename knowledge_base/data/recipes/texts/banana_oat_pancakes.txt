Name: Banana Oat Pancakes
Cuisine: American
Time: 15 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 2 ripe bananas
- 2 large eggs
- 100g rolled oats (certified gluten-free)
- 100ml milk (dairy or plant-based)
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- Butter or coconut oil for cooking
- Maple syrup, fresh berries, or sliced banana to serve
Instructions:
1. Blend oats in a food processor until they resemble coarse flour.
2. In a large bowl, mash bananas thoroughly with a fork.
3. Add eggs, milk, vanilla, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt. Mix well.
4. Stir in oat flour to form a thick batter. Let rest 2 minutes.
5. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and add a small knob of butter or coconut oil.
6. Pour small ladles of batter (about 3 tablespoons each) into the pan. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and edges look set, about 2-3 minutes. Flip and cook 1-2 minutes more.
7. Serve stacked with maple syrup, berries, or sliced banana.
Notes: The riper the banana, the sweeter and more flavourful the pancakes. These are naturally gluten-free when using certified GF oats, and nut-free. They contain eggs and may contain dairy depending on the milk used.
