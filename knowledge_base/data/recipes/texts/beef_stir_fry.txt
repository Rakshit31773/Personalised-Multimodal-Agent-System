Name: Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry
Cuisine: Chinese
Time: 20 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 400g beef sirloin or flank steak, thinly sliced against the grain
- 400g broccoli, cut into small florets
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2cm fresh ginger, grated
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce (use tamari for gluten-free)
- 2 tablespoons oyster sauce (use gluten-free version)
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- Sesame seeds to garnish
Instructions:
1. Toss sliced beef with 1 tablespoon soy sauce and cornstarch. Marinate 10 minutes.
2. Mix remaining soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, and sesame oil to make the sauce. Set aside.
3. Blanch broccoli florets in boiling salted water for 90 seconds. Drain and set aside.
4. Heat a wok over high heat until smoking. Add 1 tablespoon oil and sear beef in batches until browned, about 1-2 minutes per batch. Remove and set aside.
5. Add remaining oil, garlic, and ginger. Stir-fry 30 seconds.
6. Return beef and broccoli to the wok. Pour sauce over. Toss together for 1 minute until everything is coated and glossy.
7. Serve over steamed rice, garnished with sesame seeds.
Notes: Slicing beef against the grain is critical for tenderness. The cornstarch marinade creates a velvety coating on the meat — a technique called velveting used in Chinese cooking.
