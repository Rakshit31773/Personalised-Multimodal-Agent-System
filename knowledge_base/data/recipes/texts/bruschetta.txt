Name: Bruschetta
Cuisine: Italian
Time: 12 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: vegan, dairy-free, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 1 baguette or ciabatta, sliced into 1.5cm rounds
- 400g ripe cherry tomatoes, diced
- 2 garlic cloves (1 minced, 1 whole for rubbing)
- Large handful fresh basil leaves, torn
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
- Salt and black pepper
Instructions:
1. Preheat oven grill or a griddle pan to high heat.
2. Mix diced tomatoes with minced garlic, torn basil, olive oil, and balsamic vinegar. Season with salt and pepper. Set aside to marinate while you prepare the bread.
3. Grill bread slices for 1-2 minutes per side until golden and marked.
4. While still hot, rub the surface of each toast with the cut side of the whole garlic clove.
5. Spoon the tomato mixture generously onto each toast.
6. Serve immediately — bruschetta gets soggy quickly.
Notes: Use the ripest tomatoes available for maximum flavour. Bruschetta is vegan and nut-free. The garlic-rubbing step is essential — the heat of the toast activates the garlic oil, infusing the bread without a raw garlic taste.
