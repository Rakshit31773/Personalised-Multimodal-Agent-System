Name: Caesar Salad
Cuisine: American
Time: 15 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: vegetarian, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 2 romaine lettuce hearts, leaves separated
- 80g Parmesan, shaved
- 100g sourdough croutons (or store-bought)
- 2 egg yolks
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 4 anchovy fillets, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- 100ml extra-virgin olive oil
- Salt and black pepper
Instructions:
1. Make the dressing: whisk egg yolks, garlic, anchovies, mustard, lemon juice, and Worcestershire sauce in a bowl.
2. Slowly drizzle in olive oil while whisking continuously until the dressing emulsifies and thickens.
3. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper.
4. Place romaine leaves in a large bowl. Add dressing and toss to coat every leaf.
5. Add croutons and half the Parmesan, toss again.
6. Serve topped with remaining Parmesan shavings.
Notes: Caesar dressing contains raw egg yolks — those avoiding raw eggs can use a pasteurised egg or mayonnaise-based dressing instead. The anchovy is key to the umami depth; omit for a fully vegetarian version.
