Name: Chicken Tikka Masala
Cuisine: Indian
Time: 45 minutes
Difficulty: Medium
Dietary: gluten-free
Ingredients:
- 700g boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 200ml plain full-fat yogurt
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 2 teaspoons paprika
- 400g canned crushed tomatoes
- 150ml double cream
- 2 large onions, finely diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2cm fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- Fresh coriander to garnish
Instructions:
1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, half the garam masala, turmeric, and a pinch of salt for at least 20 minutes (overnight is better).
2. Grill or pan-fry the marinated chicken until slightly charred, about 8-10 minutes. Set aside.
3. In a large pan, heat oil over medium-high heat. Fry onions until deep golden, about 12 minutes.
4. Add garlic and ginger, cook 2 minutes. Add remaining spices and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add crushed tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes until thickened.
6. Stir in cream, add the cooked chicken, and simmer 5 minutes.
7. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve with basmati rice or naan.
Notes: For a dairy-free version, substitute coconut cream for the double cream and use a dairy-free yogurt for the marinade.
