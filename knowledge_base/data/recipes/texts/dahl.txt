Name: Red Lentil Dahl
Cuisine: Indian
Time: 35 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 300g red lentils, rinsed
- 400g canned crushed tomatoes
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2cm fresh ginger, grated
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil or vegetable oil
- 800ml vegetable stock or water
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander and lemon juice to serve
Instructions:
1. Heat oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Fry onion until golden, about 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and ginger, cook 2 minutes until fragrant.
3. Add cumin, turmeric, garam masala, and cayenne. Stir constantly for 1 minute.
4. Add rinsed lentils, crushed tomatoes, and stock. Stir to combine.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook uncovered for 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lentils are completely soft and the dahl has thickened.
6. Season with salt, squeeze in lemon juice, and stir in fresh coriander.
7. Serve with basmati rice or naan bread.
Notes: Red lentils dissolve into a creamy texture as they cook — no soaking required. This dahl is entirely plant-based, gluten-free, and nut-free, making it suitable for most dietary requirements.
