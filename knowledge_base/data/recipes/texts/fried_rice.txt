Name: Egg Fried Rice
Cuisine: Chinese
Time: 15 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, vegetarian
Ingredients:
- 400g cooked jasmine rice (day-old is best)
- 3 large eggs, beaten
- 100g frozen peas, thawed
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce (use tamari for gluten-free)
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- Salt and white pepper
Instructions:
1. Heat a wok or large frying pan over very high heat until smoking.
2. Add 1 tablespoon vegetable oil. Pour in beaten eggs and scramble until just set. Remove and set aside.
3. Add remaining oil and garlic, stir-fry 30 seconds.
4. Add cold rice. Spread in an even layer and press down to allow the bottom to crisp, about 1-2 minutes. Then stir-fry vigorously to break up clumps.
5. Add peas and spring onions. Toss 1 minute.
6. Return scrambled eggs to the wok. Drizzle with soy sauce and sesame oil.
7. Toss everything together for 1 minute. Season with white pepper.
Notes: Day-old rice is essential — freshly cooked rice is too moist and will steam instead of fry, resulting in a sticky texture. This is a quick and satisfying meal ready in 15 minutes. The eggs, tomatoes, and spring onions make this a good candidate for multi-ingredient queries.
