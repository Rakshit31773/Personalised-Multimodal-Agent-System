Name: Greek Salad
Cuisine: Greek
Time: 10 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 4 large ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 1 cucumber, roughly chopped
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 150g kalamata olives
- 200g feta cheese, cut into slabs
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 4 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
- Salt and black pepper
Instructions:
1. Combine tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and olives in a large bowl.
2. Drizzle with olive oil and red wine vinegar.
3. Season with salt, pepper, and dried oregano. Toss gently.
4. Place slab of feta on top (do not crumble — the traditional style keeps it whole).
5. Drizzle a little more olive oil over the feta and sprinkle with extra oregano.
6. Serve immediately.
Notes: Greek salad does not include lettuce in the traditional version — it is a chunky, robust salad. The feta should be served in a block, not crumbled. Use ripe, flavourful tomatoes for the best result.
