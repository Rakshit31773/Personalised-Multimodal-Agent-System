Name: Turkish Lentil Soup
Cuisine: Turkish
Time: 40 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 300g red lentils, rinsed
- 2 medium carrots, diced
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 400ml water
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1.5 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon paprika (sweet or smoked)
- 1/2 teaspoon dried mint
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Salt and black pepper
- Crispy paprika butter and fresh mint to garnish (optional, omit butter for vegan)
Instructions:
1. Heat olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Sauté onion and carrot until soft, about 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic, cumin, paprika, and tomato paste. Cook 2 minutes, stirring constantly.
3. Add rinsed lentils, stock, and water. Bring to a boil.
4. Reduce heat and simmer for 25-30 minutes until lentils are completely dissolved and the soup is smooth.
5. Use an immersion blender to blend until silky smooth, or blend in batches.
6. Stir in dried mint and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper.
7. Serve with crusty bread. Garnish with a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of paprika.
Notes: This soup freezes beautifully for up to 3 months. It is naturally thick, creamy, and entirely plant-based despite containing no dairy.
