Name: Miso Soup
Cuisine: Japanese
Time: 10 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free
Ingredients:
- 4 cups dashi stock (kombu-based for vegan)
- 3 tablespoons white miso paste
- 150g soft tofu, cut into small cubes
- 2 tablespoons dried wakame seaweed
- 2 spring onions, thinly sliced
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds (optional)
Instructions:
1. Bring dashi stock to a gentle simmer over medium heat. Do not boil.
2. Add the dried wakame seaweed and let it rehydrate for 1-2 minutes.
3. Add the cubed tofu and warm through for 2 minutes.
4. Remove the pot from heat. Ladle a small amount of hot broth into a bowl, whisk in the miso paste until fully dissolved, then stir this mixture back into the pot.
5. Taste and adjust miso quantity if needed.
6. Serve immediately in bowls, garnished with sliced spring onions and sesame seeds.
Notes: Never boil miso after adding it — boiling destroys the probiotic cultures and dulls the flavour. Kombu dashi is made by soaking a 10cm piece of dried kombu seaweed in 4 cups cold water for 30 minutes then heating to just below a boil.
