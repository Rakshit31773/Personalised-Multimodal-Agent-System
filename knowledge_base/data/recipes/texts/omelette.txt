Name: French Omelette
Cuisine: French
Time: 8 minutes
Difficulty: Medium
Dietary: vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 3 large eggs
- 15g unsalted butter
- 1 tablespoon chives, finely chopped
- Salt and white pepper
- Optional fillings: Gruyère cheese, sautéed mushrooms, or fresh herbs
Instructions:
1. Crack eggs into a bowl, season with salt and white pepper, and beat vigorously with a fork until completely homogeneous with no streaks of white.
2. Heat a 20cm non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Add butter and let it melt and foam without browning.
3. Pour in the egg mixture all at once. Immediately start stirring with a silicone spatula in fast, small circular motions while simultaneously shaking the pan.
4. When the eggs are just set and slightly creamy in the centre, stop stirring. Let the bottom set for 10 seconds.
5. Add any fillings to the centre.
6. Tilt the pan at 45 degrees and fold the omelette over itself onto a plate in one smooth motion, creating an oval shape.
7. Scatter chives on top. Serve immediately.
Notes: A French omelette should be pale yellow with no browning — the sign of a perfectly controlled heat. It is one of the quickest hot meals in the collection at only 8 minutes.
