Name: Pad Thai
Cuisine: Thai
Time: 20 minutes
Difficulty: Medium
Dietary: gluten-free, dairy-free, contains-peanuts
Ingredients:
- 200g flat rice noodles
- 200g medium shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 eggs
- 100g bean sprouts
- 3 spring onions, sliced
- 3 tablespoons tamarind paste
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce
- 1 tablespoon palm sugar or brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 50g roasted peanuts, roughly chopped (contains peanuts)
- Lime wedges and dried chilli flakes to serve
Instructions:
1. Soak rice noodles in warm water for 30 minutes until pliable but not fully soft.
2. Mix tamarind paste, fish sauce, and sugar to make the pad thai sauce. Set aside.
3. Heat a wok over high heat until smoking. Add 1 tablespoon oil and cook shrimp until pink, about 2 minutes. Push to one side.
4. Add remaining oil and garlic, stir-fry 30 seconds.
5. Add drained noodles and pad thai sauce. Toss vigorously for 2 minutes.
6. Push noodles aside, crack eggs into the wok and scramble until half-cooked, then fold into the noodles.
7. Add bean sprouts and spring onions, toss for 30 seconds.
8. Serve topped with crushed peanuts and lime wedges.
Notes: Pad Thai contains peanuts — unsuitable for those with peanut or tree nut allergies. A very hot wok is essential for authentic "wok hei" (breath of the wok) flavour.
