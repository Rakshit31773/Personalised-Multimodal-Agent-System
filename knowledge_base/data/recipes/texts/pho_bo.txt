Name: Vietnamese Beef Pho
Cuisine: Vietnamese
Time: 90 minutes
Difficulty: Hard
Dietary: gluten-free, dairy-free
Ingredients:
- 1kg beef bones (knuckle and marrow)
- 300g beef sirloin, thinly sliced
- 400g flat rice noodles
- 1 large onion, halved
- 5cm fresh ginger, halved lengthways
- 3 star anise
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 4 cloves
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- Bean sprouts, fresh basil, lime wedges, sliced chilli to serve
Instructions:
1. Char the onion and ginger halves directly over a gas flame or under a broiler until slightly blackened. This adds smokiness to the broth.
2. Blanch beef bones in boiling water for 5 minutes, drain, and rinse under cold water.
3. In a large pot, combine bones, charred onion and ginger, star anise, cinnamon, and cloves with 3 litres of water. Bring to a boil, skim foam, reduce to a low simmer.
4. Simmer broth for 60-90 minutes. Season with fish sauce and sugar.
5. Strain broth through a fine sieve. Keep hot.
6. Cook rice noodles per package directions; divide among bowls.
7. Arrange raw beef slices over noodles. Ladle boiling hot broth over the beef — the heat will cook the thin slices.
8. Serve with bean sprouts, basil, lime, and chilli on the side.
Notes: The longer you simmer the bones, the richer the broth. This is the most time-consuming recipe in the collection at 90 minutes.
