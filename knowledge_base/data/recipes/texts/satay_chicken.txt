Name: Chicken Satay
Cuisine: Malaysian
Time: 30 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: gluten-free, dairy-free, contains-peanuts
Ingredients:
- 600g chicken thigh fillets, cut into strips
- 4 tablespoons peanut butter (smooth) — contains peanuts
- 200ml coconut milk
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce (use tamari for gluten-free)
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 tablespoon honey or palm sugar
- 2 stalks lemongrass, white part only, minced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- Cucumber, red onion, and jasmine rice to serve
- Wooden skewers, soaked in water
Instructions:
1. Mix turmeric, cumin, half the garlic, and half the lemongrass with a splash of oil. Toss chicken strips in this marinade. Set aside for 15 minutes.
2. Make peanut sauce: combine peanut butter, coconut milk, soy sauce, lime juice, honey, remaining garlic, and lemongrass in a small saucepan. Simmer over low heat for 5 minutes, stirring until smooth.
3. Thread marinated chicken onto soaked skewers.
4. Grill skewers over high heat (BBQ, griddle pan, or oven grill) for 3-4 minutes per side until cooked through with char marks.
5. Serve skewers with peanut dipping sauce, sliced cucumber, and red onion.
Notes: This recipe contains peanuts and is not suitable for those with peanut or nut allergies. The peanut sauce can also be served as a dip for vegetables or with rice noodles.
