Name: Shakshuka
Cuisine: Middle Eastern
Time: 25 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 4 large eggs
- 400g canned crushed tomatoes
- 1 large onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley or coriander to garnish
Instructions:
1. Heat olive oil in a large, deep frying pan or cast-iron skillet over medium heat.
2. Sauté onion and pepper until softened, about 7 minutes.
3. Add garlic, cumin, paprika, and cayenne. Cook 1 minute, stirring constantly.
4. Pour in crushed tomatoes. Season with salt and pepper. Simmer 10 minutes until sauce thickens.
5. Make 4 wells in the tomato sauce using a spoon. Crack one egg into each well.
6. Cover and cook over low heat for 5-7 minutes, until whites are set but yolks are still runny.
7. Garnish with fresh herbs and serve directly from the pan with crusty bread or pita.
Notes: Shakshuka is a one-pan dish with eggs baked in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce. It is suitable for vegetarians. For extra richness, crumble feta cheese over the top before serving.
