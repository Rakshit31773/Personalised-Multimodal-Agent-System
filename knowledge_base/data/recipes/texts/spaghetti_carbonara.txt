Name: Spaghetti Carbonara
Cuisine: Italian
Time: 25 minutes
Difficulty: Medium
Dietary: dairy-free-unfriendly
Ingredients:
- 400g spaghetti
- 150g guanciale or pancetta, diced
- 4 large egg yolks
- 2 whole eggs
- 100g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
- 50g Parmesan, finely grated
- 2 teaspoons freshly cracked black pepper
- Salt for pasta water
Instructions:
1. Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta cooking water before draining.
2. While pasta cooks, sauté guanciale in a large frying pan over medium heat until crispy and the fat has rendered, about 5-7 minutes. Remove pan from heat and let cool slightly.
3. In a bowl, whisk together egg yolks, whole eggs, grated Pecorino, and grated Parmesan. Season with lots of black pepper.
4. Add drained pasta to the guanciale pan off the heat. Add the egg mixture and toss vigorously, adding pasta water a splash at a time to create a creamy, silky sauce.
5. Serve immediately with extra Pecorino and black pepper on top.
Notes: The key is heat control — the eggs should cook from the residual heat of the pasta, not scramble in a hot pan. Authentic carbonara contains no cream.
