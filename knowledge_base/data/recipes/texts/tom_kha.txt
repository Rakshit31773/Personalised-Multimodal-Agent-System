Name: Tom Kha Gai
Cuisine: Thai
Time: 25 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 400ml coconut milk
- 400ml chicken stock
- 400g chicken breast, thinly sliced
- 200g oyster or button mushrooms, sliced
- 3 stalks lemongrass, bruised and sliced
- 6 slices galangal (or ginger as substitute)
- 6 kaffir lime leaves, torn
- 3 Thai chillies, bruised
- 3 tablespoons fish sauce
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- Fresh coriander and spring onions to garnish
Instructions:
1. Combine coconut milk and chicken stock in a saucepan. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat.
2. Add lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime leaves, and chillies. Simmer 5 minutes to infuse aromatics.
3. Add mushrooms and sliced chicken. Cook until chicken is just cooked through, about 5-7 minutes.
4. Season with fish sauce, lime juice, and sugar. Balance to taste — it should be rich, sour, and mildly spicy.
5. Ladle into bowls and garnish with fresh coriander and spring onion.
Notes: Tom Kha Gai is a rich coconut milk soup, distinct from the clear Tom Yum. The galangal, lemongrass, and lime leaves are aromatic infusers — push them to the side before eating. For a vegan version, use tofu and replace fish sauce with soy sauce.
