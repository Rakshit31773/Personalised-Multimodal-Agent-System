Name: Tom Yum Soup
Cuisine: Thai
Time: 20 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Dietary: gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free
Ingredients:
- 1 litre chicken or vegetable stock
- 200g medium shrimp, peeled
- 150g oyster mushrooms, torn
- 2 stalks lemongrass, bruised and cut into 5cm pieces
- 4 kaffir lime leaves, torn
- 3cm galangal, sliced
- 3 tablespoons fish sauce
- 3 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 tablespoon palm sugar or brown sugar
- 2 Thai chillies, bruised
- 100g cherry tomatoes, halved
- Fresh coriander to garnish
Instructions:
1. Bring stock to a boil in a medium saucepan.
2. Add lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime leaves, and chillies. Simmer 5 minutes to infuse.
3. Add mushrooms and cherry tomatoes. Cook 3 minutes.
4. Add shrimp and cook until pink and curled, about 2-3 minutes.
5. Season with fish sauce, lime juice, and palm sugar. Taste and balance sweet, sour, salty, and spicy.
6. Ladle into bowls and garnish with fresh coriander.
Notes: Tom yum is a clear hot and sour Thai broth — do not blend it. The aromatic ingredients (lemongrass, galangal, lime leaves) are not meant to be eaten; push them to the side. For a vegan version, use vegetable stock and replace fish sauce with soy sauce.
